2023 - Cameron Brown (76 pts)

Current Champion: Cameron Brown
CamBro went back-to-back, taking the Ron in both '22 and '23.
His 76 in 2023 is the highest winning score since Ben Murrill's record 81 in 2018.
 
2022 remains the only tie in Darwin Decathlon history. Cameron and Terry Register both finished on 65 points, and Terry earned the heads-up challenge for the title — but the winds proved too strong in washers.

The annual champion gets to remove one of the 10 decathlon events and replace it with an event of their choice
for the following year's competition. Past winners have enjoyed the prestige of proudly displaying the trophy
on their mantle, using it as collateral for large loans, or simply as a way to get into the
most exclusive night clubs.

Hopefully this year's champion will be tossing the trophy between boats during the victory parade.
