Win Probability

Each line is one competitor's chance of taking the title, tracked through the tournament. Everyone opens at 10%. Watch the field collapse as the events fall.

How this is computed: these odds are simulated, not recorded. Nobody tracked live odds in 2015. After each event we take the real standings and play out every remaining event 8,000 times at random, then count how often each player ends up on top. The shape is real; the percentages are a model.

Three eras, three homes. 2015–2019 was scattered across the Katy and West Houston suburbs; 2020 was cancelled outright; 2021 onward moved everything under one sky at Vacek Ranch.

Private property (Bateman House, Vacek Ranch) is shown as an approximate area, never a pinned address — see DISCLAIMER.md. Stars Sports Bar (2015, Golden Tee / Shuffle Board / Darts) was never geocoded and isn't mapped, rather than guess at a location.

The Field Guide

Every event ever contested in the Darwin Decathlon — the ten that survive, and the ones that were voted off. Each has its own page: rules as they evolved, venue history, all-time records, and who actually owns it.

Scoring, in general: the event winner takes 10 points, 2nd takes 9, on down to 1 for 10th. A missed event scores 0. Pair events split the band — the winning team takes (10+9)/2 = 9.5 each. The current leader picks their partner first, then 2nd place, and so on; with an odd number of players the last one picked plays solo.
