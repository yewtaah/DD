## What This Is

The Darwin Decathlon rewards survival of the fittest through a grueling ten-event
competition. Whether it's home run derby, washers, or beer pong, every event demands
the utmost mastery of decidedly non-athletic pursuits. While some have questioned the
event as grown men simply acting like children, contestants know this is a noble
endeavor unlike any other in human history.

Each event awards **10 points to the winner**, 9 for second, on down to 1 for tenth.
Miss an event and you score nothing. Pair events split the band &mdash; the winning
team takes **(10+9)/2 = 9.5 each**. Most points out of 100 is Grand Champion, takes
home the Ron, and earns the right to **delete one event and replace it with anything
they want** for the following year.

That last rule is why no two tournaments have ever been the same.

**The constants.** Skeet shooting has opened every single tournament since 2015 &mdash;
as the tradition goes, *good to get the shotguns put away before the beers come out.*
Home Run Derby and Beer Pong have appeared in all six.

**The arrivals.** Disc Golf debuted in 2019. The Long Drive and a country
"shooting gallery" off the back patio arrived in 2021. Kickball replaced Shuriken in 2023.

**The departures.** Golden Tee, Darts, Go Karts, Field Goal Kicking, TopGolf and
Shuffleboard have all had their run and been voted off.

**The exile and return.** Shuriken &mdash; throwing stars and knives &mdash; was played
in 2015, **banned in late 2015 over neighbor concerns**, and returned in 2021 to the
wide-open safety of the Vacek Ranch. Ben Murrill called his "double shot" on a
high-scoring star and took the 10 points for top ninja. He was wearing sneakers.

## The Venues

Three eras, three homes.

**Katy & West Houston (2015 &ndash; 2019).** The tournament was scattered across the
suburbs &mdash; skeet at American Shooting Center, home run derby at George Bush Park
and later West Oaks Little League, TopGolf out on I-10, field goals at Cinco Ranch
High School, and everything else in the Bateman backyard. In 2019 Rosenberger
Construction opened up their office for a Friday night of washers and shuffleboard,
which was, by unanimous account, awesome.

**The gap (2020).** Cancelled. COVID.

**Vacek Ranch, Texas hill country (2021 &ndash; present).** The whole tournament under
one sky. Lodging, food, drinks and game supplies for a small entry fee &mdash; show up
and compete. It also made a few things possible that a suburban cul-de-sac never
could, throwing stars chief among them.

## The SNAFU

Every event carries a lurking penalty for the bottom of the field:

| Event | Fail condition | Consequence |
|:------|:---------------|:------------|
| Skeet Shooting | Under 10 targets | Shotgun a beer |
| Home Run Derby | Don't clear the infield on the fly | Wear a pink tutu |
| Darts | Under 20 points | Drink a cosmopolitan martini |
| Shuffleboard | Score zero | Goldschlager shot |
| Golden Tee | Land in the water | Grape Ape shot |
| TopGolf | Hit the red target | Jaeger shot |
| Disc Golf | Hole in one | *Everyone else* takes a shot |

Beer Pong runs its own economy: each player gets one **"blue ball"** per game, called
before the shot. Make it and you pick a second cup for the opposition.

## The Numbers

The **Chronicles** section of the site carries every scorecard since 2015 &mdash;
simulated win probability through each tournament, career arcs across the years, and
the anomalies buried in the math.

Some things the data turned up:

- **Ben Murrill finished 8th of 10 in 2015 with 42 points.** Three years later he put
  up 81, still the best winning score anyone has managed.
- **Cameron Brown owned disc golf, then lost it to the Vaceks.** He won it in 2019 and
  2021 &mdash; the 2021 recap called him the only winner the event had ever had. Then
  Duane and Dan Vacek tied for it in 2022, and Duane took it outright in 2023.
- **Andy Conacher won the inaugural tournament in 2015 and finished runner-up in 2023**,
  eight years and a whole change of era apart.
- **Scott Bateman shot a perfect 25 in skeet at the 2021 tournament** using hand
  throwers &mdash; and still finished 4th.
- **Four of the ten 2015 event columns don't add up.** Every event should distribute
  exactly 55 points; Skeet totals 46, Darts and Golden Tee 51, TopGolf 54. Recorded
  as found, uncorrected.

### A note on data and privacy

This is a public repository. **Real rosters and scorecards containing contact
information are gitignored and never committed** &mdash; only `*.template.csv` files,
which use dummy data, are tracked. Player names appear in published standings;
emails, phone numbers and home addresses do not, anywhere.

Every historical figure here is transcribed from an original scorecard and
cross-checked against a second source. Where a scorecard's printed total disagrees
with the sum of its own cells, **the printed total wins** and the discrepancy is
documented rather than quietly corrected. See
[`data/UNVERIFIED-claims.md`](data/UNVERIFIED-claims.md) for the full verification
log &mdash; including the claims that didn't survive it.
