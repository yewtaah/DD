General Rules

10 events. 10 players. Event winner takes 10 points, 2nd takes 9, on down to 1 point for 10th. A missed event scores 0. Most total points out of 100 is Grand Champion. Ties split the point band — two players tied for 1st each take 9.5.

Pair events: the current points leader picks their partner first, then 2nd place, and so on. Players may not pick anyone in the top half of the standings. With an odd number of players, the last-place player plays solo — natural selection.
